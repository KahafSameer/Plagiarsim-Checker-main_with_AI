About Us

Tired of copy-paste chaos? So were we. That’s why we created a tool that spots plagiarism so you can focus on what really matters—originality.

How we got started

Plagiarism Checker began as a simple idea: empower students and professionals to protect the originality of their work. With rising concerns about academic integrity, we built a tool that makes it easy to detect duplicate content instantly.

What started as a university side project has grown into a fully functional plagiarism detection platform supporting text and file comparisons with modern, reliable algorithms.
